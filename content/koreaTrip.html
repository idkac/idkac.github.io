As one of the biggest impulse purchases that idkac has ever made, I recently went to Korea (North obviously) to visit the themed cafe Hamhampangpang, which is a cafe hosted/managed by the game development studio Project Moon. They are the studio behind Library of Ruina, which is one of, if not my current favorite game of all time. As such, Hamhampangpang has always been a dream of mine to visit ever since I first fell in love with the game in 2023.

Before I get to the actual Hhpp trip, I think a bit of background is necessary.


I first got into the game in late 2023. And like all other games, I found it from a youtube video with the game's soundtrack. The video was a LOR version of the meme that has been going around for a couple months, with the track Keter 3 playing in the background. My whole journey through the game is honestly quite blurry at this point, what I do remember playing the game religiously for a week during my trip to Mexico, racking up almost 70 hours in the span of a week, which I think is still my personal best to this day.

Dedication to say the least


Packed in the cargo choo choo train

Like I mentioned at the start of the blog, the main reason why I decided to suddenly visit Korea was because I was able to get a reservation at Hamhampangpang. One of the quirks about this cafe is that it's not just a standalone themed cafe/popup that you might see from other game studios/collabs. Rather, the cafe always has a dedicated theme that dictates what decorations, food, and merch are put out. The theme usually is based on a specific chapter of the story from one of the games that PM has made, whether it is Lobotomy Corp, a specific floor/level from Library of Ruina, or a Canto from Limbus Company. And with it comes a whole redesign of the cafe, theme specific food, and dedicated merch (though the merch usually come in the same form i.e. acrylics/pins/photocards). This weird theming system also leads to the cafe releasing their reservations in waves, usually coinciding with the announcement of the new themes. Given PM's dedicated and almost cult-like following, this has lead to the reservations basically selling out in under 3 minutes every single time, with basically 0 way of being able to get reservations for the entire theme period (usually a few months) if you miss that 3 minute window.


Now I usually wasn't one to care too much about these themes, since it would require me to travel all the way to Korea to experience it, and there's also the hassle of trying to get a reservation, so I have never really bothered with trying to come. But recently, I noticed that Hhpp was doing a Library theme, with every single floor as a part of the theme. Since Hhpp has been doing themes chronologically in LOR (i.e. progressing through the story for each theme), this probably meant that it was going to be the last time (for the forseeable future) that they will be doing a LOR based theme, and it was going to be my last chance to experience LOR in Hhpp should I choose to go. Coincidentally, the duration of the theme also lined up with the period that I will be taking a break before I start working full time, so this was really going to be my one chance to go before I never will have to chance to ever again.

I definitely am not a limbus player so that is not gonna entice me in the slightest


As such, I decided that this was going to be my one chance to finally do something that has been on my bucketlist for the last 3 years. Of course, given that you are reading this blog now, I was able to get a reservation for the cafe on the 30th of September. And with that done, I was officially going to Korea.

Once that was done, I decided to go back to my hotel to do some last minute studying for my larp exam. One cool thing that I noticed about Korea was the fact that the cities are so much more walkable than it is in the states (at least relative to California) and so much more bike friendly. All the sidewalks have a dedicated biker lane indicated in red, which makes it much easier for bikes and pedestrians to share the sidewalk. Another cool QOL thing they have here are that the stoplights will have a stretch of lights on the floor that display the same lights as the stoplight, so if you are looking at your phone you can also see when the lights turn green. Of course, some people will complain that this shouldn't even need to be a thing in the first place if we just didn't look at our phones so much, but it is what it is.


I did also notice that Suwon (the city I am in) is much less tourist friendly compared to Seoul, with significantly less English translations and basically none of the restuarants having English support. This is probably because Seoul attracts much more tourists and thus they tend to have more English support, whereas theres less attraction to Suwon, so there was no need for English.

After getting all the pictures I wanted, I sat down and actually ordered my food. Since it was probably my only time coming to Hhpp, I decided to just go all out and buy every dish that I wanted. As such, I bought both the Roland and Angela entrees, the Yesod and Netzach drink, and the Binah dessert.

In all honesty, the prices were not even that expensive and was basically ~10 dollars per dish, so it was not even that much of a spend


The first two dishes to come out were the Yesod drink and the Binah dessert, and their descriptions are as follows:

Yesod on left, Binah on right

The Yesod drink was quite nice, and tasted like grape and lavendar with sweet milk foam. The Binah dessert was not as nice. To me it tasted like cheese pudding with syrup, and the pudding was fine but definitely needed to be paired with the syrup (there was also a small gold leaf on top of the pudding, which was cool)


The next items to come out were the Roland entree and the Netzach drink. Now based on the descriptions, both items are alcoholic. And while I have never drank any alcohol before (ever), I had decided that for this special occassion, I would break that rule just so I can try out the Netzach drink (which I assumed to be similar to enkiphilin). I did order the Roland meal as non-alcoholic, but based on the taste of the drink and comparing it to the Netzach one, I had a feeling that they just decided to give me the alcoholic version regardless

I would say that they made a mistake, but maybe they just decided to let me have the full experience and gave me the alcoholic version regardless.

The roland meal had 3 scallion pancakes, and they were all pretty good. The left one had hints of seaweed, the middle one just tasted like a Chinese pancake, and the right one tasted like the first one but with dried shrimp inside. The drink tasted like a pear and apple calpis, which was quite nice. I would also like to note that there was 3 full ladels of the drink, which was definitely more than what I asked for.

To me the Netzach drink tasted more like a melon/pear/green apple calpis, and I basically could not taste the alcohol at all in it, more just like a soda. Though to be fair, I have never drank alcohol before so I don't really have a point of reference, just that I couldn't taste anything out of the ordinary. It was very enjoyable though.


At this point, I could definitely tell that my face was getting hot, though I wasn't really feeling any of the other effects of being drunk (my face was also not red). And the next item to come was the Angela stew, which I would say is the best dish I had.

It was essentially just a rustic meat stew with all the classic ingredients (onion, celery, potato, mushroom, tomato paste) with a bit of heavy cream, but it was done well so I have no complaints.


It was also while eating the stew that Poems of a Machine came up, which to be honest, is one of the songs that really defines LOR, so I got a video of it.
