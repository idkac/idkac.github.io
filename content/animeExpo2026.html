Day 2

So for day 2, mostly just the same as before. Try and find merch, go to cosplay getherings, and go to panels. I spent the majority of the morning going walking around the artist alley again, and the first panel that I went to was the You and I are polar opposites Season 2 premier with 2.6 hog rider. The panel was pretty good, and I was genuinely surprised by the number of attendees present at the panel (the gender split was also something among the lines of 60/40, which I was also surprised by). I have actually never watched the anime before, since I had already read the manga to completion, but I was happy that the adaptation was faithful to the source material, and the animation quality was quite nice. Overall, I am just happy that a manga I liked was getting the recognition that it deserves.

In fact, I am now inspired to watch the anime, which would also give me a bit of a refresher on what happened in the manga

Otherwise, not a lot of pictures here. One notable thing is that the executive project director of ba Kim Yong-ha and the rest of the ba development team showed up during the meetup for pictures. Keep in mind that these guys are korean developers with high executive positions, and they just decided to randomly show up to a (mostly) unofficial cosplay meetup for the game. BA might not do a lot of things perfectly, but the one thing that I highly respect the dev team for is the amount of passion and dedication they have for the game. Despite being such a big game, the dev team still takes time out of their day to do this kinda stuff. In fact, as far as I am aware, blue archive is the only game that has their developers and directors just show up randomly to events. Kim Yong-ha especially works double overtime with these collaberations and events, and I am pretty sure he shows up to most of them whether its in korea or overseas.

So remember how I mentioned at the start of the blog that the fact that I was driving down was going to be important? Well here's the context for that. Since the drive to LA was going to take ~6-7 hours, we collectively decided to make a dedicated road trip playlist on spotify that we can listen to while on the road. And as you know, I am a big music enjoyer (idkac.com), so of course I went demon mode on the playlist and I ended up adding like ~360/700 of the songs in the playlist, even though we had 6 people contributing to it. As such, the songs that were added by people who were not named idkac and 2.6 hog rider were colloquially named "golden scars" (as they basically added 10 to 20 songs each out of the 700). Funnily enough, we were actually lucky to pull some of these songs on our trip, and it was very funny to have the speaker randomly start blasting Kanye music after a stream of jpop/vocaloid songs.

In addition, to spice up the playlist a little, I also decided to add a few golden scars of my own to the playlist in the form of Thick of It by KSI and Mongolian Throat Singing. Unfortunately we were not able to hit Thick of It, but we were blessed with the good fortunes of 10 minutes of Mongolian Throat Singing, which I did happen to catch on video:


Other than that, it was a pretty uneventful day 4. Overall, the trip was quite enjoyable as always, and having more company definitely made it more fun. I also cut down on spending this time since I went wayyyy too crazy last year due to impulse purchases, so I am quite happy with that as well. Whether I show up to 2027 we will have to see, as the management of the con for this year left a lot to be desired. If you were keeping up with the news of ax for this year, you would have known that this year was definitely way more mismanaged than previously, with much less staffing and much more overcrowdness. They also shrunk the artist alley by half this year, which I was not a fan of as many of the artists that I have seen in the previous 2 cons were not here this year, many of whom had merch that I would have loved to buy (specifically 2 artists who sold LOR merch). Honestly, whether I show up next year will heavily depend on what seasonal animes/games will be released that summer, which would compell me to show up to attend their panels (unfortunately 100 wasn't doing a panel for this year, which really sucked).
